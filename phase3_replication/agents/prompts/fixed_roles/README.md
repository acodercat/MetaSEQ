# Role prompts of the MetaSEQ topology (code name `multi_agent_fixed_roles_verified`)

`published/` holds the texts of the group's released multi-agent harness
(`benchmark/adapters/multiagent_adapter.py`: `WORKER_ROLES`,
`_SHARED_DISCIPLINE`, `_COORDINATOR_SYS`) verbatim; a test compares them byte for
byte. `stripped/` holds the same texts with the analysis knowledge removed, so
that a worker knows no more about the analysis than the single agent it is
compared with. `diff.md` records every change (regenerated by the tests).

## Stripping rule

Kept (what a team needs to run):
- each role's identity and what it owns; the plan format and the coordinator's
  planning heuristics (which roles a task needs);
- the shared-runtime discipline: who resolves entities, never overwrite another
  agent's variables, how to flag a doubt;
- the verifier's decision rule (recompute independently, reassign when the
  values differ beyond rounding) and its `VERIFY` print line;
- the `RESOLVED:` / `UNRESOLVED:` print lines (a handover convention).

Removed (what the single agent is not told either):
- how any quantity is computed: standard definitions (sum, max, mean, count,
  Pearson), units and scale, PostGIS usage (`ST_*`, geography vs geometry);
- lookup tactics: exact match then one fuzzy fallback, query limits per entity;
- sanity checks and thresholds: row counts, empty or zero results, factors of
  x100 / x1000 / 24, plausibility of magnitudes;
- error catalogues (entity id, time window, units, sum vs peak, ...);
- examples that carry domain content (`company_id`, `district_id_jingxiu`).

`synthesis_report` contains no analysis knowledge and is unchanged. In
`knowledge_retrieval` the examples of what to gather (AQI/AQHI breakpoints, WHO and
national limits, units) are replaced by "the standards and thresholds it relies on".
In the coordinator and `data_acquisition`, "the database" is made source-neutral,
since the Beijing data are frames, and the coordinator's "Maximum 6 steps" is spelled
as what it means, at most six entries in the plan (2026-10-06).

## Adaptations to this suite (both prompt sets)

The harness's 90 tasks were single-turn and stated their outputs in a
"store the results in: ..." sentence. This suite's tasks are mostly multi-turn and
declare their outputs as typed variables, so, exactly as the MetaSEQ variant of
the harness did: the required outputs are the declared variables registered so
far (the single agent's runtime lists the same); every worker is shown the
conversation so far under the harness's own "EXACT CONVERSATION CONTRACT" and
"TURN RULES" wording (minus its rule about NOW() and today's date, which is
analysis advice); and the verifier reads "raw data" rather than "raw database",
since the Beijing tasks hold their data in frames. The mechanics -- plan
parsing, the fixed fallback plan, the step budget, the type-only manifest -- are
the released harness's, compared with it in the tests.

Lab changes to how the instructions are assembled (2026-10-06, `docs/deviations.md` §15):
the required outputs are the current turn's own (never the environment's variables, and
in a turn-by-turn task not outputs completed earlier); the staged-values manifest leaves
out the environment's data; every worker is told that its role's rules take precedence
over the suite system prompt's single-analyst guidance (`precedence.md`); and without a
verifier, synthesis is not told that one will check its values. The step reserves are arm
settings (`synthesis_steps`, `verify_steps`). Since 2026-10-08 (§17) the verifier's
staged-values heading points it at the REQUIRED list it was given and names the other
values as context (the harness ran one turn, where "verify EACH" meant every value), and the
manifest is read after the turn's registrations have landed.
